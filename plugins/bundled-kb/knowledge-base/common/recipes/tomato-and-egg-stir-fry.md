# Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry

## Ingredients

- 3 large eggs
- 2 medium ripe tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons cooking oil, divided
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, divided
- 1 teaspoon sugar, optional
- 1 green onion, thinly sliced, optional

## Preparation

1. Cut each tomato into bite-size wedges.
2. Beat the eggs with half of the salt.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a frying pan or wok over medium-high heat.
4. Add the eggs. Gently push the cooked edges toward the center until the eggs are softly set, then transfer them to a plate.
5. Add the remaining oil and the tomatoes to the pan. Cook for 2 to 4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes soften and release some juice.
6. Add the remaining salt and the optional sugar.
7. Return the eggs to the pan. Fold everything together for about 30 seconds, breaking the eggs into large pieces.
8. Top with green onion and serve immediately, usually with steamed rice.

## Notes

- Ripe tomatoes provide more juice and flavor.
- Remove the eggs while they are still soft because they will cook again when returned to the pan.
- For a saucier dish, add 2 tablespoons of water while cooking the tomatoes.

## Allergen note

Contains egg. Check every packaged ingredient when cooking for someone with a food allergy.
