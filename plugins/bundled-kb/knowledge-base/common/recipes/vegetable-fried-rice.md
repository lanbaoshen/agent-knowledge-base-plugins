# Vegetable Fried Rice

## Ingredients

- 3 cups (about 450 g) cooked and chilled rice
- 2 tablespoons neutral cooking oil
- 1 small carrot, finely diced
- 1/2 cup frozen peas
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced, white and green parts separated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil, optional
- Black or white pepper, to taste

## Preparation

1. Break up any clumps in the chilled rice before cooking.
2. Heat a large frying pan or wok over medium-high heat.
3. Add the cooking oil, carrot, peas, and the white parts of the green onions. Stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Add the garlic and cook for about 20 seconds, just until fragrant.
5. Add the rice. Stir and toss for 3 to 4 minutes until it is hot and the grains are separated.
6. Pour the soy sauce around the edge of the hot pan, then toss it through the rice.
7. Add the optional sesame oil, pepper, and green parts of the onions.
8. Taste, adjust the seasoning, and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Chilled rice is drier than freshly cooked rice and separates more easily in the pan.
- Keep the ingredients moving and avoid crowding a small pan. Cook in two batches when necessary.
- Add other quick-cooking vegetables such as corn, diced bell pepper, or chopped mushrooms.
- Refrigerate cooked rice promptly and reheat it until steaming hot.

## Allergen note

Soy sauce contains soy and often wheat. Use products appropriate for the diner's allergies and dietary needs.
