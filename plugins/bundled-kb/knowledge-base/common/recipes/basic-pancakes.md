# Basic Pancakes

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/4 cups (300 ml) milk
- 1 large egg
- 3 tablespoons melted butter or neutral cooking oil
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, optional
- A little butter or oil for the pan

## Preparation

1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In another bowl, whisk the milk, egg, melted butter, and optional vanilla.
3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry mixture. Stir just until no dry flour remains. A few small lumps are fine.
4. Heat a lightly greased frying pan or griddle over medium heat.
5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter into the pan for each pancake.
6. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface and the edges begin to look dry, about 2 minutes.
7. Flip once and cook the other side until golden, about 1 minute.
8. Serve warm with fruit, yogurt, butter, or syrup.

## Notes

- Overmixing can make the pancakes dense or chewy.
- Adjust the heat if the outside browns before the center cooks.
- This recipe makes about 8 to 10 medium pancakes.

## Allergen note

Contains wheat, milk, and egg. Check every packaged ingredient when cooking for someone with a food allergy.
